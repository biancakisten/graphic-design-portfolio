Fred's Coffee — Logo Design

A coffee-shop logo project presented through the Fred's Coffee logo artwork. The lesson introduces logo types, concept creation and creating a coffee-shop logo in Adobe Illustrator.

Project overview

The lesson's coffee-shop challenge asks students to create a rough logo concept, identify possible words, icons and colours, and create a coffee-shop logo using Adobe Illustrator. This page presents the supplied Fred's Coffee logo artwork.

Lesson context

The lesson introduces symbol/icon, wordmark, letter mark, combination mark and emblem logo types, and encourages keeping a logo simple. Its coffee-shop examples include a cup or steam, coffee beans and a coffee stain.

Deliverable

Fred's Coffee logo artwork, supplied as a scalable SVG file.

Project asset

The page uses the existing Fred's Coffee logo SVG from the portfolio assets.

Project overview

The project requires designing a professional business card for Fred's Coffee, using the coffee-shop branding developed in an earlier lesson. The design should communicate the business identity clearly while presenting essential contact information in an attractive and organised layout.

Lesson context

This lesson focuses on business card design, layout, typography and print specifications. Students apply graphic design principles to create a business card that reflects an existing brand identity. Particular attention is given to standard business card dimensions, readability and appropriate font sizes.

Deliverable

A professionally designed, print-ready Fred's Coffee business card, incorporating the coffee shop's logo, brand colours, typography and relevant business contact details.

Project asset

The project uses the existing Fred's Coffee logo artwork developed in Module 1, Lesson 3. The logo is supplied as a scalable SVG file and incorporated into the business card design.
